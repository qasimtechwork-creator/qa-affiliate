# Temu Affiliate Program: Payouts & Pakistan Specifics — Research Notes

Researched 2026-10-02. Every fact below carries its source URL. Temu's rules are
market-specific; where the official policy and regional sources disagree, both are
recorded and the disagreement is flagged.

## 1. Payout / withdrawal methods for a Pakistan-based affiliate

- Official Temu affiliate policy (Exhibit A, "Affiliate Earnings Terms and Conditions",
  last updated Jun 11 2026): "Available Earnings" can be withdrawn either as
  **Temu Credits** to the Temu account, or as **cash via PayPal**.
  Source: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_policy.html (params stripped)
- Temu's official affiliate FAQ: "Available payout methods depend on your country
  and account setup. Check the specific options shown on your withdrawal page for
  what applies to you." Withdrawal methods and thresholds "differ by country and
  payout method".
  Source: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_question.html
- Regional guides report that Temu runs local versions of the affiliate program for
  Pakistan with rewards shown in local currency, and that "reward amounts,
  withdrawal minimums and payout methods, such as **bank transfer or Temu Credit**,
  are set per market".
  Source: https://reverbico.com/blog/the-temu-affiliate-program-a-complete-guide-to-boosting-your-income/
- **Critical for Pakistan: PayPal does not operate in Pakistan.** As of 2026,
  PayPal offers no personal or business accounts for Pakistani residents and no
  local bank withdrawals (banned since 2009; repeated launch promises never
  materialized). A Pakistani affiliate therefore cannot receive the cash-via-PayPal
  payout with a Pakistani PayPal account.
  Sources:
  - https://wise.com/pk/blog/paypal-account-in-pakistan
  - https://webstudy.pk/paypal-account-in-pakistan/
  - https://news.cxoforum.global/how-pakistani-freelancers-can-receive-international-payments-in-2026/
- Practical consequence: for a Pakistan-registered account the realistic options
  are Temu Credits and whatever the Withdraw page lists for the PK market
  (per-market guides mention bank transfer). The user must check their own
  Withdraw page; Temu does not publish the PK method list publicly.

## 2. Minimum withdrawal threshold, schedule, fees

- Official policy (US/global page): cash withdrawal only if the "Available"
  balance is **over $20**; no withdrawal under $20; **the full balance must be
  withdrawn — partial withdrawals are not permitted**.
  Source: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_policy.html
- Thresholds vary by market (Mexico: MX$300; UAE/Saudi: SAR80 triggers the
  PayPal-link popup). One QA Affiliate note records a **$10** threshold on our
  account's market terms — the exact figure must be read from the account's own
  Withdraw page.
  Sources:
  - https://www.thefreelibrary.com/Temu+Affiliate+Program%3a+Share+and+Earn+Cash+From+Home!-a0783933687
  - https://uaetoday.com/news_details/76987/index.asp
- Schedule: commission is credited to the "Pending" balance **2 days after** the
  qualifying order is placed; it moves to "Available" only after order delivery
  is confirmed plus a verification period (the period is shown on the Program
  page and varies by commission type).
  Sources:
  - https://www.temu.com/affiliate_policy.html
  - https://www.temu.com/affiliate_question.html
  - One affiliate support-center version states transfer to the available account
    "30 days after delivery"; a UAE-market guide says "3 days after delivery".
    The program page's stated period governs.
- Payout processing: PayPal withdrawals reported at 3–5 business days; Temu
  Credits are instant.
  Source: https://medium.com/@eakjotkaur85/how-i-started-earning-with-the-temu-affiliate-program-93d34743c754
- Fees: Temu's policy publishes no withdrawal fee of its own. Any PayPal-side
  receiving/currency fees would apply on PayPal's end (not verified for PK
  because PayPal is unavailable there).

## 3. Where to add payout / bank details in the dashboard (step by step)

Per Temu's official affiliate FAQ ("Withdraw & Payments"):

1. Sign in to Temu (temu.com or the app) with the affiliate account.
2. Open the **Affiliate page** (search "affiliate" on temu.com or tap the
   affiliate entry in the app's account center).
3. Open the **Withdraw** section.
4. Choose the preferred payout method from the options shown for your market.
   To link PayPal (where offered): follow the on-screen prompt to provide and
   verify the PayPal account (some markets also ask for mobile-number
   verification).
5. If a method is already set, use the **Change** option in the Withdraw section
   to switch it.
6. Track requests under the **Withdrawal History / records** tab in the same
   section.

Source: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_question.html

Note: only one active withdrawal request at a time is usually allowed, and daily
request limits may apply (exact limits shown in the account).

## 4. Is temu.com geo-blocked in Pakistan? What does a PK visitor see on temu.to/k links?

- **temu.com is NOT IP-blocked in Pakistan.** In Aug 2025 Pakistan's Competition
  Commission (CCP) asked the PTA to ban Temu, but its own letter stated Temu was
  "operational in Pakistan … accessible nationwide". No ban has taken effect;
  Temu even serves a Pakistan storefront (temu.com/pk-en).
  Sources:
  - https://www.brecorder.com/news/40379935
  - https://tribune.com.pk/story/2563454/ccp-seeks-ban-on-temu-e-commerce-platform
  - https://www.temu.com/pk-en/terms-of-use.html
- The reported symptom — clicking a temu.to/k/... product link from a Pakistani
  IP shows "product not available" / an error instead of the product — is
  **consistent with Temu's regional catalogs**: product availability, pricing
  and assortment differ per country storefront. Links generated from one
  region's storefront may not resolve to the same product on another region's
  site. (This is the likely explanation; no source was found that documents
  the exact error text for cross-region temu.to links.)
- Important nuance for commissions: the program is **geofenced**. Temu's policy
  states affiliate benefits "may vary depending on the country/region in which
  such Temu User resides", and regional guides warn that a US-program signup
  generally only earns on US-based users' purchases.
  Sources:
  - https://www.temu.com/affiliate_policy.html
  - https://www.allblogthings.com/2025/12/temu-affiliate-program.html

## 5. Any known problem with a Temu affiliate account created/used from a Pakistani IP?

- **No known ban or defect specific to Pakistani-IP accounts.** Pakistan appears
  on Temu's own official affiliate-region list (affiliate_regions page) and on
  multiple 2026 eligible-country lists (77–88 countries including Pakistan).
  Sources:
  - https://www.temu.com/affiliate_regions.html (region list page; Pakistan listed)
  - https://www.dozro.com/digital-marketing/list-of-temu-countries
  - https://siliconafrica.org/list-of-temu-affiliate-program-countries/
  - https://www.way2earning.com/2025/09/temu-affiliate-program/
- Caveats that do apply:
  - The US version of the affiliate policy limits eligibility to US residents;
    Temu runs separate regional programs. The account must be registered under
    the **correct country/site** (Pakistan). Temu's support center: switch sites
    via the country button (PC: next to the cart; app: profile → personal
    center → switch sites) and the affiliate page auto-registers the account for
    that region.
    Source: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_support_center.html
  - Changing country/region on Temu "may cause disqualification from the
    Program" (official policy). Keep the affiliate account on one region.
  - Temu may require a **Traffic Verification Process** (submit channel links,
    screenshots) before earnings/withdrawals unlock; failing it suspends the
    account and forfeits pending balances.
    Source: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_policy.html
  - Commissions are primarily for **new users**: rewards depend on the referred
    user qualifying as an eligible new user and completing required actions in
    time.
    Source: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_question.html

## 6. "Convert link" product links: attribution window and crediting

- Temu advertises an **extended 30-day cookie/attribution window** for affiliate
  links ("30-Day, No Missed Sales").
  Sources:
  - https://www.temu.com/affiliate_recruit.html (recruit page; "Extended Cookie Window, 30-Day")
  - https://www.shop-today-online.com/2026/09/join-temu-affiliate-program-and-earn.html
- Official affiliate FAQ: a referred user has **30 days to place a qualifying
  order** and earn the affiliate commission — **but only if they redeemed the
  affiliate code within 1 hour of downloading the app**. If the code is redeemed
  more than 1 hour after first download, the referral does not qualify.
  Source: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_question.html
- The recorded terms for the QA Affiliate account match: **20% commission on
  orders within 30 days of the referral's app download**.
  Source: https://sainazeemtech.com/earn-money-online-with-temu-affiliate-program/ (same 20%/30-day structure)
- Crediting flow: qualifying order → commission in Pending (2 days after order
  in policy; ~5 minutes in some market guides) → Available after delivery
  confirmation + verification period → withdrawable. Cancelled/returned orders
  forfeit the commission.
  Sources:
  - https://www.temu.com/affiliate_policy.html
  - https://www.way2earning.com/2025/09/temu-affiliate-program/

## Bottom line for the QA Affiliate / Qasim situation

1. Pakistan is an eligible affiliate market; a PK-created account is not known
   to be defective.
2. The realistic payout routes for a Pakistan account are **Temu Credits** and
   whatever the account's own Withdraw page lists (market guides mention bank
   transfer). **Cash via PayPal is effectively closed to Pakistani residents**
   because PayPal does not operate in Pakistan.
3. Withdrawal threshold per official policy is **$20 minimum, full balance only**
   (market-specific figures vary; check the account's Withdraw page — our
   account notes say $10).
4. The "product not available" symptom from Pakistani IPs is most plausibly the
   **regional-catalog mismatch** (US-generated product links vs the PK
   storefront), not an IP block — temu.com is accessible from Pakistan.
5. Attribution: 30-day window; commission credited after the referred user's
   qualifying order, available after delivery confirmation.
