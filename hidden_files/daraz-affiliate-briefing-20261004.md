# Daraz Affiliate Program — Master Briefing for Qasim
**Date:** 2026-10-04 | **Prepared for:** Qasim (Pakistan-based creator)
**Purpose:** Evaluate Daraz affiliate as a Pakistan-focused revenue stream (payments land directly in Pakistan).

---

## 1. Program Status — Does It Exist in 2026?

**YES.** The Daraz Affiliate Program is live and operating in Pakistan in 2026, run by Daraz (Alibaba Group). It is the largest local affiliate opportunity in Pakistan.

- **Official signup URL:** https://affiliate.daraz.pk
- **Affiliate dashboard:** called **"Adstream"** (Daraz Adstream Portal) — this is where links are generated and earnings tracked.
- Free to join. Open to Pakistani creators (bloggers, YouTubers, TikTokers, Facebook/Instagram influencers, WhatsApp broadcasters).

## 2. How to Join — Requirements & Approval

1. Go to https://affiliate.daraz.pk and click "Join Now" / "Sign Up".
2. Fill in: full name, email, phone number, CNIC number (Pakistan), province/city, postal address.
3. Declare your promotion platform(s): blog, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp, etc. (with links/handles).
4. Select monthly traffic size and traffic type.
5. Submit and wait for approval — **24–48 hours typical, up to 3–5 working days**. Approval confirmation arrives by email.
6. Once approved, log in to the Adstream dashboard.

**Tip from experienced affiliates:** adding an active TikTok/social account at signup speeds up approval.

## 3. Commission Rates (per category)

Rates vary by product category (Daraz sets these; subcategories may differ):

| Category | Commission |
|---|---|
| Electronics | 1% – 3% |
| Fashion & Beauty | 5% – 12% |
| Home & Living | 4% – 10% |
| Sports & Outdoor | 5% – 11% |
| Groceries & Pets | 2% – 8% |

- Overall cited range across sources: **4% – 14%**, commonly "up to 11%".
- High-ticket electronics pay low % but high volume; fashion/beauty pay the best rates.
- Commissions are paid in **PKR** on the net sale amount, only on **confirmed, non-refunded orders**.

## 4. Cookie Duration — IMPORTANT DISCREPANCY

- **Blogger/guide sources claim:** 30 days.
- **Daraz's official affiliate agreement** (published on daraz.com.np and daraz.com.bd, same Alibaba legal template used across markets): **Clause 4.2.3 — cookies valid for 7 days**.
- **Attribution:** last-click wins (the affiliate in the last position of the click chain before purchase gets credit).
- **Recommendation:** assume **7 days** until the Pakistan dashboard/terms confirm otherwise. The 30-day figure is unverified marketing copy.

## 5. PAYMENT — The Critical Section

### Methods available in Pakistan (confirmed by multiple sources)
- **Direct bank transfer** (any Pakistani bank)
- **JazzCash**
- **EasyPaisa**

Payment method is chosen during registration / in the dashboard.

### Minimum payout threshold (conflicting reports — verify in dashboard)
| Source claim | Threshold |
|---|---|
| way2earning.com | $25 (~PKR 7,000) |
| jobrealinfo.com | ~$20 |
| truehost.cloud | PKR 5,000 (bank) / PKR 1,000 (EasyPaisa/JazzCash) |

Most likely: **~$20–25 equivalent**, possibly lower for mobile wallets. Confirm inside the Adstream dashboard after signup.

### Schedule & terms
- **Monthly payouts** — paid on/before the 15th (some sources say 20th) of the following month.
- Only **confirmed, non-refunded, non-cancelled** orders count. Returns/chargebacks are deducted.
- Earnings below threshold **roll over** to the next month (not lost).
- Real-time earnings/clicks/conversions visible in the dashboard Reports section.

### Bottom line for Qasim
Unlike Amazon (no Pakistan bank payouts) and Temu/AliExpress (USD-centric), **Daraz pays directly into Pakistani banks and mobile wallets in PKR**. This is its single biggest advantage for a Pakistan-focused project.

## 6. Generating Product Links (Adstream Dashboard)

Three ways (per the official Adstream dashboard guide):

1. **From the Daraz app:** log in with your affiliate email in the Daraz app, open any product, copy the product page URL, and promote it (attribution via your account).
2. **Campaigns:** in the Adstream portal, click **Promote** → pick a running campaign (trending, bestselling, high-order deals). Daraz provides creative support for campaigns.
3. **Custom link:** paste any Daraz product page URL into the **Target URL** field → **Generate Link** → copy your unique affiliate URL.

Dashboard also shows: clicks, conversions, earnings, conversion rate — updated in real time.

## 7. Product Feed / API

- **No official public affiliate API** found for programmatic product pulls.
- The dashboard provides **banners and product feeds** (per affiliate guides) for manual use.
- Unofficial option: open-source scrapers exist (e.g., GitHub `daraz-scraper` uses Daraz's internal search API) — but scraping violates Daraz ToS if abused; use only the dashboard for link generation.
- **Practical implication:** unlike Temu/AliExpress bulk workflows, Daraz affiliate is a **manual, dashboard-driven** process — pick products, generate links one by one (or via campaigns). No bulk CSV import path confirmed.

## 8. Where Can Links Be Posted? (Rules)

Explicitly allowed per guides and the program's design:
- Blogs / niche websites (reviews, buying guides, "best of" lists)
- Facebook (posts, groups)
- Instagram (stories, bio)
- TikTok (product videos)
- YouTube (video descriptions)
- WhatsApp (status, groups, broadcasts)
- Telegram channels
- **Pinterest — YES.** Two independent guides explicitly list Pinterest: "Run Pinterest pins with links" and "Pinterest Pe Shopping Post Karna — post product image + price + benefits, link in pin description."

**Restrictions:** no self-referrals, no fraud/duplicated/automated referrals (Daraz reserves the right to invalidate suspicious transactions). Last-click attribution applies. Standard affiliate honesty rules (disclose affiliate links).

**Note for Qasim's current setup:** his standing rule keeps affiliate products OFF YouTube/Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn (tech channels) — Daraz links would follow the same placement logic: Pinterest + website/blog + any future Pakistan-focused site.

## 9. Real User Experiences — Do Pakistanis Actually Get Paid?

- **No affiliate-specific payment-failure complaints found** in 2026 sources. (Complaints found online concern Daraz *shopping/sellers* — late deliveries, counterfeit claims — not affiliate payouts.)
- Anecdotal: a Karachi affiliate reported growing from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 45,000+/month via Facebook recommendations (unverified, from a guide blog).
- General consensus across Pakistani earning guides: Daraz affiliate is **legit and beginner-friendly**, with monthly PKR payouts via bank/JazzCash/EasyPaisa.
- **Caveat:** commission rates are modest (1–3% on electronics), so volume or high-% categories (fashion/beauty) matter.
- way2earning.com verdict (2026): "Yes — for beginners. But not ideal for scaling long-term income."
