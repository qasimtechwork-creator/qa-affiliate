About QA Affiliate

Qasim Kayani — Founder, QA Affiliate

Hi, I'm Qasim Kayani.

I'm an AI content creator and video editor, and the founder behind QA Affiliate. I started this platform with a simple idea: discovering great women's products shouldn't feel like guesswork.

QA Affiliate curates trending women's fashion, beauty, skincare and lifestyle products from trusted merchants like Amazon, AliExpress and Temu. Every collection is built from real product research — merchant data, ratings, price tracking and editorial review — and every "View Deal" button is an affiliate link, clearly disclosed.

How we make money: when you buy through our links, we may earn a commission from the merchant. It never changes the price you pay, and it never decides what we recommend. Our Affiliate Disclosure explains this in full.

Our promise: no fake reviews, no invented discounts, no paid placements disguised as picks. Just transparent product discovery.
