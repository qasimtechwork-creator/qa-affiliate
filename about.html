About QA Affiliate

Qasim Kayani — Founder, QA Affiliate

Hi, I'm Qasim Kayani.

I'm an AI content creator and video editor, and the founder behind QA Affiliate. I started this platform with a simple idea: discovering great women's products shouldn't feel like guesswork.

QA Affiliate curates trending women's fashion, beauty, skincare and lifestyle products from trusted merchants. Every collection is built from real product research — merchant data, ratings, price tracking and editorial review — and every "View Deal" button is an affiliate link, clearly disclosed.

How we make money: when you buy through our links, we may earn a commission from the merchant. It never changes the price you pay, and it never decides what we recommend. Our Affiliate Disclosure explains this in full.

How we choose products

  • Signal collection. We gather merchant bestseller indicators, ratings momentum, review counts and price movement for products in our categories.
  • Editorial review. No product is listed automatically. We check real availability, accurate descriptions and fit with our audience before it appears.
  • Honest presentation. We show verified ratings and sold counts where the merchant publishes them. Where we can't verify a price, we say "Price varies — see live deal" instead of inventing one.
  • Ongoing refresh. Products that go out of stock, lose momentum or lose affiliate eligibility are demoted or removed.

What we will never do

  • Never publish fake reviews, invented ratings or fabricated discounts.
  • Never accept payment for product placement disguised as a recommendation.
  • Never use AI-generated or stock photos and present them as product images.
  • Never hide our affiliate relationships — disclosure is on every page that carries product links.

Our promise: transparent product discovery, researched properly and presented honestly. If you have a question or a product suggestion, we'd love to hear from you.
