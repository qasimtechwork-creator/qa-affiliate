Cookie & Analytics Notice

Last updated: September 28, 2026

QA Affiliate keeps tracking minimal. Here is exactly what is stored in your browser and why.

What we store

  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4): privacy-conscious measurement with IP anonymization, used to understand which pages and collections are useful. Analytics only loads once a measurement ID is configured — before that, no analytics requests are made at all.
  • Local storage: your browser may locally store anonymous affiliate-click events and newsletter signup queues so site features keep working. This data stays on your device; only aggregated analytics reach us.
  • No advertising cookies are set by QA Affiliate itself.

Merchant cookies

When you click through to a merchant such as Temu, that merchant sets its own cookies under its own privacy policy — including the cookies that attribute your purchase to our affiliate link. We have no control over merchant cookies; check the merchant's policy for details.

Managing cookies

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time — the site remains fully usable without them.
  • Clearing local storage removes locally kept click and signup records from your device.
  • Note: blocking merchant cookies may prevent affiliate attribution, which is how the site is funded — but it will not affect the prices you see.
