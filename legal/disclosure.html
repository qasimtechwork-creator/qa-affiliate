Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: September 28, 2026

QA Affiliate is reader-supported. When you buy through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission from the merchant. Our affiliate links are currently active through the Temu affiliate program; we may add other merchant programs in the future and will update this page when we do.

What this means for you

  • You pay the same price whether you use our link or go to the merchant directly. The commission comes out of the merchant's margin, not your wallet.
  • How tracking works: our links contain a referral identifier. When you click through and make a qualifying purchase — typically within 30 days of visiting — the merchant attributes the sale to us.
  • Commissions support our work: product research, editorial content, and operating costs.

Our editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not determine which products we feature or how we describe them. Collections are built from merchant data, ratings and editorial review — we do not accept payment for placement, and where we show pros and cons, both are our honest assessment based on available product information and customer feedback signals.

Where you'll see disclosure

  • A disclosure strip appears on every page that carries product links.
  • Every product detail page includes an affiliate disclosure box next to the purchase button.
  • All outbound merchant links use rel="nofollow sponsored" so search engines and browsers treat them transparently.

Display advertising

Separate from affiliate links, we may in the future show display advertising. That is covered by our Advertising Disclosure and is currently not active.
