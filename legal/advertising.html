Advertising Disclosure

Last updated: September 28, 2026

QA Affiliate's primary revenue is affiliate commissions (see our Affiliate Disclosure). In the future we may also display advertising, for example through Adsterra or a similar provider. Display advertising is currently not active on this site.

Our advertising principles

  • Clear labeling: any advertising will be visually distinct from editorial content and product picks, and labeled as advertising.
  • No influence: advertisers do not and will not influence product collections, ratings, reviews or recommendations.
  • No interference: we will not run advertising that blocks content, hijacks navigation, or degrades the product discovery experience.
  • Relevance and taste: ad categories will be kept appropriate for our audience.

Affiliate links vs. advertising

These are different things on QA Affiliate. Affiliate links are the "View Deal" buttons that take you to a merchant — disclosed on every page that carries them. Display advertising would be separate banner or native ad placements — none exist on the site today. If that changes, this page will be updated first.
