Pinterest Pipeline

The Pinterest pin pipeline is run automatically by the assistant every day and is not operated from this panel — this admin console is for catalog management only.

Every catalog product gets two pins: one pin links to the product page on this website, and the second links straight to the merchant affiliate link. Pins are published immediately as public pins (never scheduled) and are paced to respect Pinterest's daily publishing limits, in the same product order, day after day.

Product pin images are the product images as-is with zero editing. Assistant-designed creative is reserved for tech and AI content pins only.
