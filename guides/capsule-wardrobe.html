The idea in one paragraph

A capsule wardrobe is a small set of versatile pieces that all work together. Twelve well-chosen items can produce dozens of outfits — less decision fatigue, less waste, more wear per piece.

The 12 pieces

  • Tops (4): white tee, black tee, striped long-sleeve, silk blouse
  • Bottoms (3): dark jeans, tailored trousers, midi skirt
  • Layers (3): blazer, cardigan, trench or wool coat
  • Shoes (2): white sneakers, black ankle boots

How to shop it

Stick to a tight color palette — black, white, navy, beige plus one accent. Prioritize fit over trends; a $40 blazer that fits beats a $200 one that doesn't. Buy in this order: bottoms first (they anchor outfits), then layers, then tops, then shoes.
